# GrapeRoot Enterprise Discovery

A product-management case study and working toolkit for moving an AI developer tool from individual adoption toward enterprise demand.

This repository documents the business cofounder perspective: customer discovery, pilots, reports, license access, feedback synthesis, willingness-to-pay questions, enterprise positioning, and product requirements. It does not claim ownership of GrapeRoot's technical context architecture.

## What this demonstrates

- Segmenting individual developers, team champions, engineering leaders, and economic buyers.
- Turning pilot conversations into testable requirements.
- Designing a pilot with explicit success criteria and stop/continue decisions.
- Separating evidence from assumptions and polite interest.
- Balancing enterprise requests against self-serve simplicity.
